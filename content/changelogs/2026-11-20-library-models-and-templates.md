title: Library cards and model management
category: Library
summary: Manage eligible AI models from their cards, review mission references before removal, and preview mission templates without leaving the Library.
---

- Edit AI model details from the card or its action menu when your Workspace role allows it. Models managed by SkyTrack or another member remain read-only.
- Before removing a model used by missions, review the affected missions and projects in the warning.
- Open a mission template in a preview dialog, then close it or use it to create a mission.
- Library cards now use consistent owner attribution, clearer personal model labels, and aligned card actions. Cards without an available action no longer show an interactive hover border.
- Dialog footer actions use the default button size for a consistent layout.

---
date: 2026-11-18
title: Mission Map rendering controls
category: Settings
summary: Tune Mission Map rendering quality in Preferences and see a more balanced Mission Insights overview.
---

- Choose Performance, Balanced, or High quality for Mission Map rendering in Preferences. The selection is saved and applied to the active map.
- New missions no longer inherit the sample collaborators used by seeded shared missions.
- Mission Insights Overview now balances the run summary and media panel across desktop widths.
- The Preferences drawer aligns its title and close control in a single header row.
